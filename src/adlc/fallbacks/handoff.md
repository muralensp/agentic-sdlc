Leave a resume note so a fresh session can continue.

1. State the goal and the current status in two or three lines.
2. List what is done, what is next, and what is blocked, with the reason.
3. Link to the files, commits, branches, logs and pull requests that matter; do not copy their contents.
4. Record decisions made and the ones still open.
5. Keep it short enough to read in a minute.
