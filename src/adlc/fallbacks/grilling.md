Interview the person until nothing important is assumed. A person must be present.

1. Read the repository context first (CLAUDE.md, ADRs, glossary, the board). Look up facts yourself; never ask the person for something you can find.
2. Map the decisions as a tree. Ask every question whose prerequisites are already settled, in one numbered round.
3. For each question give your recommended answer, worded so that "yes" accepts it.
4. After the answers, recompute which questions are now open and ask the next round.
5. Stop when no open decision remains, say so, and wait for the person to confirm the shared understanding before acting on it.
