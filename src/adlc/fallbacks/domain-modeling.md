Keep the project's language and decisions written down. A person must be present.

1. Read GLOSSARY.md and the existing ADRs before proposing anything.
2. When a new concept or term appears, propose a glossary entry: one name, one plain definition, and the words it replaces.
3. When a decision was made that is hard to reverse, propose an ADR: context, decision, alternatives, consequences.
4. Propose only. The person accepts or rejects each entry; never write one unasked.
5. Use the project's own terms in everything you write afterwards.
